## Operational Clauses

### [SOP-v2025-1.1] Risk-Based Budget Allocation & Dispatch Prioritization
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Field dispatch capacity shall be allocated strictly according to machine learning fusion risk rankings under a monthly inspection budget constraint of top 5% of client population ($k \le 0.05$). Flagged accounts within this top 5% risk tier must be dispatched for on-site forensic inspection within fourteen (14) calendar days of score generation.
* **Mandatory Action:** High-Priority Inspection Dispatch (Level 2). Automated dispatch ticket routed to specialized fraud inspection brigades.
* **Evidence Required:** Machine learning risk dossier showing customer in top 5% risk percentile (`risk_rank_pct <= 0.05`) with documented SHAP top-5 feature drivers.

---

### [SOP-v2025-1.2] Mandatory Dual-Fuel Cross-Inspection Protocol
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** On any premises supplied with dual utility connections (`has_elec == 1` and `has_gaz == 1`), a fraud flag generated on either meter triggers an automatic, mandatory forensic audit of both meters within 48 hours. If the electricity meter exhibits tampering, the natural gas counter must undergo acoustic bypass leak testing and seal inspection during the same inspection visit.
* **Mandatory Action:** Mandatory Dual-Counter Audit. Comprehensive inspection of electricity meter, gas counter, and incoming service lines.
* **Evidence Required:** Dual-meter forensic inspection report signed by certified utility enforcement officer.

---

### [SOP-v2025-2.1] Acute Year-on-Year Consumption Collapse
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** An active account that records a drop in monthly consumption (`cons_pm`) exceeding 50% year-on-year (`yoy_drop_max >= 0.50`) across two (2) or more consecutive billing periods, without formal written notice of occupancy termination, shall be classified as presumed physical meter bypass or magnetic tampering.
* **Mandatory Action:** Urgent Forensic Site Inspection (Level 2). On-site audit required within 14 calendar days.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing record documenting two consecutive cycles with $\ge 50\%$ drop relative to historical seasonal median.

---

### [SOP-v2025-2.2] Rapid Zero-Consumption Detection Threshold
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** An active client account that records two (2) or more consecutive billing invoices with total declared consumption equal to zero (`cons_pm == 0` or `zero_run_max >= 2`) shall automatically trigger an immediate physical inspection order. The district agency shall not permit subsequent billing cycle generation without on-site verification.
* **Mandatory Action:** Priority Inspection & Service Verification. Inspect for unauthorized neutral disconnection, direct bypass, or meter jamming.
* **Evidence Required:** Two consecutive zero-consumption invoices and electronic site verification report.

---

### [SOP-v2025-2.3] Zero-Tolerance Index Reconciliation Discrepancy
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Any discrepancy between the calculated index advance ($\text{new\_index} - \text{old\_index}$) multiplied by `counter_coefficient` and the total declared consumption (`consommation_level_1` through `_4`) exceeding 50 kWh or occurring across more than one invoice (`recon_mismatch >= 1`) constitutes prima facie evidence of data manipulation or meter defect.
* **Mandatory Action:** Immediate Technical Field Inspection. Check for meter register gear stripping, internal jumper manipulation, or reader transcription fraud.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing reconciliation report showing `recon_mismatch >= 1` or `recon_gap_mean > 50`.

### [SOP-v2025-3.1] Negative Counter Index Progression & Immediate Seizure
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Any instance where a current meter reading is lower than the previous reading (`new_index < old_index`, triggering `is_negative_delta == 1`) constitutes an emergency security violation. Field officers are authorized to immediately seize and replace the meter without prior administrative warning. The removed meter must be placed in a tamper-evident evidence bag and transferred to the regional forensic testing laboratory for internal mechanical examination.
* **Mandatory Action:** Immediate Emergency Seizure & Forensic Autopsy (Level 3). Meter confiscation, installation of certified smart replacement, and police referral.
* **Evidence Required:** Handheld terminal photo of reversed dials, chain-of-custody laboratory transfer form.

### [SOP-v2025-3.2] Suspected Anomaly Flag Rapid Response
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Where a field meter reader records an anomaly remark or assigns status `counter_statue == 5` (suspected anomaly / tampering flag), an enforcement team must inspect the premises within seven (7) calendar days.
* **Mandatory Action:** Priority Enforcement Dispatch (Level 2). Verify seal integrity, terminal links, and check for external magnet attachment.
* **Evidence Required:** Reader exception log recording `counter_statue 5` and completed forensic site audit sheet.

### [SOP-v2025-3.3] Unauthorized Service Reconnection Enforcement
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Any active energy consumption recorded on an account formally classified as cut off or suspended (`counter_statue == 1` and `cons_pm > 0`) shall be treated as criminal energy theft. Enforcement teams shall execute immediate physical service drop disconnection at the pole or distribution box, accompanied by a municipal police escort, and initiate judicial recovery of back-tariffs and statutory fraud penalties.
* **Mandatory Action:** Service Termination, Police Escort & Judicial Prosecution (Level 3). Physical cable removal at pole, criminal report filing, and retroactive maximum tariff penalty.
* **Evidence Required:** Live voltage measurement on cut-off meter, photographic proof of broken security padlock or bypass fuse.

### [SOP-v2025-4.1] Restriction on Consecutive Estimated Readings
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** No customer installation shall remain on estimated billing (`reading_remarque == 8`) for more than three (3) consecutive billing cycles. If an actual reading cannot be obtained on cycle 3, the account is automatically locked against further estimation and flagged for supervisor inspection.
* **Mandatory Action:** Systemic Estimation Lockout & Supervisory Audit. Mandatory physical inspection within 10 business days.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing record demonstrating three consecutive `reading_remarque 8` entries.

### [SOP-v2025-4.2] Inaccessible Meter Enclosure Directive
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Where two (2) consecutive visits record `reading_remarque == 9` (inaccessible / obstructed meter), the utility shall issue a mandatory legal notice requiring customer access within 10 business days. If access is not provided, the utility is legally empowered to obtain an administrative judicial warrant for forced entry or execute remote feeder disconnection.
* **Mandatory Action:** Legal Warrant Notice & Mandatory Relocation Order. Notice of planned service disconnection if access is denied.
* **Evidence Required:** Two consecutive reader exception logs citing `reading_remarque 9`.

### [SOP-v2025-5.1] Digital Forensics & Benford's Law Route Audits
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Algorithmic forensic screening under Benford's Law is formally integrated into operational audit protocols. Where an account or meter-reading route exhibits a Benford Mean Absolute Deviation `benford_mad > 0.05` alongside a rounded reading fraction `round_reading_share > 0.35`, the regional directorate shall initiate an internal supervisory audit of the assigned meter reader for potential collusion and falsification of field records.
* **Mandatory Action:** Internal Supervisory Audit & Route Verification. Independent supervisory team re-reads entire reader route within 5 days.
* **Evidence Required:** Benford's Law anomaly report and digit distribution histogram.

### [SOP-v2025-5.2] Commercial Tariff Reclassification & Penalty Assessment
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Where an account registered under residential tariff (`client_catg 11`) exhibits consumption exceeding two (2) times the regional residential median and peer residual `peer_resid_mean > 2.0`, an on-site commercial inspection is mandatory. If commercial machinery or business activity is verified, the account shall be retroactively reclassified to professional tariff (`tarif_type 15` or `40`) with back-billing applied across the preceding 36 months.
* **Mandatory Action:** Retroactive Tariff Reclassification (Level 2). Mandatory commercial contract adjustment and 36-month recovery invoice.
* **Evidence Required:** Commercial activity photo documentation, connected load audit sheet, and peer-residual anomaly score.

### [SOP-v2025-6.1] Mandatory On-Site Physical Evidentiary Standards
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN`
* **Clause Text:** Every on-site inspection brief must be accompanied by the following mandatory evidentiary items: (1) dual-clamp ammeter line-to-load current readings ($I_{\text{in}}$ vs $I_{\text{out}}$), (2) Gaussmeter magnetic residual measurement reading, (3) minimum of four (4) geo-tagged, time-stamped digital photographs showing cabinet, terminal block, seals, and dial faceplate, and (4) completed Inspector Brief checklist matching the official JSON audit schema.
* **Mandatory Action:** Certified Evidentiary File Compilation. Incomplete documentation invalidates inspection penalty recovery.
* **Evidence Required:** Four geo-tagged photos, dual-clamp ammeter log, and signed Inspector Brief document.
