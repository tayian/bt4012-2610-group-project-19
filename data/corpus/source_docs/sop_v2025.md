## Operational Clauses

### [SOP-v2025-1.1] Risk-Based Budget Allocation & Dispatch Prioritization
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Field dispatch capacity shall be allocated strictly according to machine learning fusion risk rankings under a monthly inspection budget constraint of top 5% of client population ($k \le 0.05$). Flagged accounts within this top 5% risk tier must be dispatched for on-site forensic inspection within fourteen (14) calendar days of score generation.
* **Mandatory Action:** High-Priority Inspection Dispatch (Level 2). Automated dispatch ticket routed to specialized fraud inspection brigades.
* **Evidence Required:** Machine learning risk dossier showing customer in top 5% risk percentile (`risk_rank_pct <= 0.05`) with documented SHAP top-5 feature drivers.

### [SOP-v2025-1.2] Mandatory Dual-Fuel Cross-Inspection Protocol
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** On any premises supplied with dual utility connections (`has_elec == 1` and `has_gaz == 1`), a fraud flag generated on either meter triggers an automatic, mandatory forensic audit of both meters within 48 hours. If the electricity meter exhibits tampering, the natural gas counter must undergo acoustic bypass leak testing and seal inspection during the same inspection visit.
* **Mandatory Action:** Mandatory Dual-Counter Audit. Comprehensive inspection of electricity meter, gas counter, and incoming service lines.
* **Evidence Required:** Dual-meter forensic inspection report signed by the inspector.

### [SOP-v2025-2.1] Acute Year-on-Year Consumption Collapse
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** An active account that records a drop in monthly consumption (`cons_pm`) exceeding 50% year-on-year (`yoy_drop_max >= 0.50`) across two (2) or more consecutive billing periods, without formal written notice of occupancy termination, shall be classified as suspected meter bypass or magnetic tampering.
* **Mandatory Action:** Urgent Forensic Site Inspection (Level 2). On-site audit required within 14 calendar days.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing record documenting two consecutive cycles with $\ge 50\%$ drop relative to historical seasonal median.

### [SOP-v2025-2.2] Rapid Zero-Consumption Detection Threshold
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** An active client account that records two (2) or more consecutive billing invoices with total declared consumption equal to zero (`cons_pm == 0` on consecutive invoices; `zero_run_max >= 2`) shall automatically trigger an immediate physical inspection order. The district agency shall not permit subsequent billing cycle generation without on-site verification.
* **Mandatory Action:** Priority Inspection & Service Verification. Inspect for unauthorized neutral disconnection, direct bypass, or meter jamming.
* **Evidence Required:** Two consecutive zero-consumption invoices and electronic site verification report.

### [SOP-v2025-2.3] Index Reconciliation Discrepancy Above 50 kWh
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Where the index advance ($\text{new\_index} - \text{old\_index}$, with meter rollovers unwrapped) multiplied by `counter_coefficient` differs from the total declared consumption (`consommation_level_1` through `_4`) by more than 50 kWh on any invoice (`recon_gap > 50`), the discrepancy shall be treated as a possible sign of data manipulation or meter defect. Smaller nonzero gaps (`flag_recon_mismatch` with `recon_gap <= 50`) are logged but do not trigger this clause.
* **Mandatory Action:** Immediate Technical Field Inspection. Check for meter register gear stripping, internal jumper manipulation, or reader transcription fraud.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing reconciliation report showing at least one invoice with `recon_gap > 50`.

### [SOP-v2025-3.1] Negative Counter Index Progression & Immediate Seizure
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Any instance where a current meter reading is lower than the previous reading (`new_index < old_index`, `flag_index_backwards`), not explained by a meter rollover past 99999 (`flag_index_rollover`), shall be treated as an emergency. Field officers may to immediately seize and replace the meter without prior administrative warning. The removed meter must be placed in a tamper-evident evidence bag and transferred to the regional forensic testing laboratory for internal mechanical examination.
* **Mandatory Action:** Immediate Emergency Seizure & Forensic Autopsy (Level 3). Meter confiscation, installation of certified smart replacement, and police referral.
* **Evidence Required:** Handheld terminal photo of reversed dials, chain-of-custody laboratory transfer form.

### [SOP-v2025-3.3] Unauthorized Service Reconnection Enforcement
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Where consumption is recorded on invoices carrying `counter_statue == 1` (`cons_pm > 0`; status assumed to mean cut-off, see `[CODE-counter_statue-1]`) and the account's service-order history confirms the service was cut off, the case shall be treated as suspected energy theft. Enforcement teams shall execute immediate physical service drop disconnection at the pole or distribution box, accompanied by a municipal police escort, and refer the case for recovery of back-tariffs and penalties.
* **Mandatory Action:** Service Termination, Police Escort & Referral (Level 3). Physical cable removal at pole, incident report, and back-billing.
* **Evidence Required:** Live voltage measurement on cut-off meter, photographic proof of broken security padlock or bypass fuse.

### [SOP-v2025-5.1] Digital Forensics & Benford's Law Route Audits
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Benford's Law screening is part of the audit protocol in this version. Where an account or meter-reading route shows a first-digit Benford mean absolute deviation `benford_mad > 0.015` (Nigrini's nonconformity band, [SRC-07]) alongside a rounded-reading share `round_reading_share > 0.40` (project heuristic: twice the 0.20 expected if last digits were uniform), the regional directorate shall initiate an internal supervisory audit of the assigned meter reader for potential collusion and falsification of field records.
* **Mandatory Action:** Internal Supervisory Audit & Route Verification. Independent supervisory team re-reads entire reader route within 5 days.
* **Evidence Required:** Benford's Law anomaly report and digit distribution histogram.

* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2025` | `valid_from: 2025-01-01` | `valid_to: None` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Where an account in `client_catg 11` (assumed residential; see `[CODE-client_catg-11]`) shows consumption above two (2) times the regional median for that category and peer residual `peer_resid_mean > 2.0` (in `features_v2`), an on-site commercial inspection is mandatory. If commercial machinery or business activity is verified, the account shall be retroactively reclassified to a professional tariff (for electricity, `tarif_type 15`, whose professional meaning is assumed; note that `tarif_type 40` is the main gas tariff, not a professional one) with back-billing applied across the preceding 36 months.
* **Mandatory Action:** Retroactive Tariff Reclassification (Level 2). Mandatory commercial contract adjustment and 36-month recovery invoice.
* **Evidence Required:** Commercial activity photo documentation, connected load audit sheet, and peer-residual score.
