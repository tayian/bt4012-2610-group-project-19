## Operational Clauses

### [SOP-v2023-1.1] General Inspection Dispatch Prioritization
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2023` | `valid_from: 2023-01-01` | `valid_to: 2025-01-01` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Field inspection scheduling shall be determined using monthly risk rankings produced by the risk model. Customers ranked within the top 10% risk tier shall be scheduled for an on-site physical visit within 45 calendar days of report publication, subject to regional vehicle availability.
* **Mandatory Action:** Routine Priority Dispatch (Level 1). Field officer creates physical work order in regional dispatch book.
* **Evidence Required:** Documented customer billing history showing inclusion in top decile risk listing.

### [SOP-v2023-1.2] Joint Multi-Meter Premises Inspection

* **Clause Text:** Where an account holds both electricity (`ELEC`) and natural gas (`GAZ`) contracts at the same physical address, inspection of one meter requires a simultaneous visual examination of the adjacent secondary counter, even if only one meter was flagged by automated screening.
* **Mandatory Action:** Dual-Counter Field Audit. Inspect seals and index progression of both counters.
* **Evidence Required:** Dual-meter inspection verification sheet signed by field inspector.

### [SOP-v2023-2.1] Acute Year-on-Year Consumption Collapse
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2023` | `valid_from: 2023-01-01` | `valid_to: 2025-01-01` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** An active account that exhibits a year-on-year drop in monthly consumption (`cons_pm`) exceeding 70% across three or more consecutive billing periods, without prior formal notification of premise vacancy, shall be classified as suspected phase-bypass tampering.
* **Mandatory Action:** Priority Inspection Dispatch (Level 2). Site inspection required within 30 days.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing transcript demonstrating three consecutive periods with $\ge 70\%$ YoY drop relative to the preceding 24-month baseline.

### [SOP-v2023-2.2] Prolonged Zero-Consumption Inactivity
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2023` | `valid_from: 2023-01-01` | `valid_to: 2025-01-01` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** When an electricity or natural gas meter records four (4) or more consecutive billing invoices with total consumption equal to zero (`cons_pm == 0`) while the customer contract remains active, the local district office must dispatch an investigator to verify premise occupancy.
* **Mandatory Action:** Occupancy Verification & Meter Seal Audit. If premise is occupied, inspect for direct bypass or magnet slowdown.
* **Evidence Required:** Four consecutive zero-consumption invoices and on-site verification of human occupancy.

### [SOP-v2023-2.3] Consumption and Index Delta Reconciliation Gap
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2023` | `valid_from: 2023-01-01` | `valid_to: 2025-01-01` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Where the numerical difference between the current index (`new_index`) and prior index (`old_index`) multiplied by the counter coefficient diverges from total declared billed consumption by more than 200 kWh (`recon_gap > 200`), the discrepancy shall be flagged for administrative review.
* **Mandatory Action:** Administrative Review & Secondary Billing Reconciliation. Field inspection scheduled if discrepancy persists over two billing cycles.
* **Evidence Required:** Billing record showing index difference discrepancy exceeding 200 kWh.

### [SOP-v2023-3.1] Negative Counter Index Progression
* **Metadata:** `doc: sop` | `version: v2023` | `valid_from: 2023-01-01` | `valid_to: 2025-01-01` | `jurisdiction: TN` | `synthetic: true`
* **Clause Text:** Any billing record where the newly recorded index is lower than the preceding index (`new_index < old_index`, `flag_index_backwards`) and the drop is not explained by a meter rollover past 99999 (`flag_index_rollover`) must be flagged as an invalid entry. A supervisory review must evaluate whether this represents reader transcription error before field dispatch. If the index reversal is verified, the meter shall be slated for replacement within 30 days.
* **Mandatory Action:** Supervisory Validation followed by Standard Replacement. Meter replacement scheduled with routine technician crew.
* **Evidence Required:** Certified billing statement showing negative index delta and supervisor sign-off.
